Phone interaction rules

These apply when Platform.isPhone is true. Tablets keep the desktop layout. Orientation comes from the view's own size, not the screen's.

Gestures

Tap a nodeSelects it and shows the peek card. Double-tap a nodeOpens the details sheet. It never opens the note. Long-pressAfter 500 ms, if the finger moved less than 8 px, opens the actions sheet. It suppresses the iOS callout and the text selection. Tap empty graphDeselects the node and closes the peek card. During a review walk, it does nothing. Drag / pinchOne finger pans and two fingers zoom. The zoom buttons and the minimap are hidden. Drag a nodeOff by default, so panning never moves a node by mistake. Turn on Edit layout under ⋯ to drag nodes. Open noteOnly from the Open note button, in the peek card, a sheet or the actions sheet.

Two kinds of surface

Modal sheet
  • Used for: details, smells, actions, the kill-and-activate confirm, and the status and date pickers.
  • A 55% scrim. A tap on the scrim, a swipe down (portrait), a swipe right (landscape) or the system back gesture closes the sheet.
  • Portrait: a bottom sheet, at most 78% of the view's height. Landscape: a full-height sheet on the right, 430 px wide including the inset.
  • The header and the footer stay fixed and the body scrolls. Overscroll is contained, so the graph behind it never pans.
  • It covers the toolbar and Obsidian's navbar. Only one sheet is open at a time: opening another replaces it.
  • When a field gets focus, the sheet grows to full height and scrolls that field above the keyboard.
Docked card
  • Used for: the peek card and the review walk.
  • It has no scrim, so you can still pan, zoom and tap the graph.
  • Portrait: along the bottom, above the navbar. Landscape: on the right, from under the header down to just above the navbar.
  • Its node is panned into the free area so the card never covers it.

Insets

  • Bottom: the height of Obsidian's mobile navbar plus a 12 px gap. Measure it from the navbar element at runtime. If that fails, fall back to env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 64 px.
  • Sides: env(safe-area-inset-left / right), plus 8 px. This is what clears the notch in landscape.
  • Measure again on resize, on orientation change and when the keyboard opens or closes.

Toolbar and sizing

  • Portrait: a row of Fit · Smells (with the count) · Review walk · ⋯. Landscape: the same buttons, icon-only, in a rail on the left.
  • ⋯ holds Ultimately serves, Add card, Edit layout and Lock positions.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 px. Inputs use a 16 px font so iOS doesn't zoom in on focus.
  • Fields go one per row. Only date pairs share a row. Status chips scroll sideways.

React Flow props

  • zoomOnDoubleClick={false} and onNodeDoubleClick open the details sheet.
  • nodesDraggable={editLayout}, panOnDrag, zoomOnPinch.
  • Long-press comes from a pointer timer on the node wrapper. onNodeContextMenu runs the same action for Android.
